Blind test runs the rating test from the sound-bed research note: 12 trials, two drills under six beds (one of them silence), shuffled, with the bed never named until the results. Watch, then tap one number — that tap both scores the trial and starts the next one. Scores are kept on this phone, so a reload carries on where you stopped.

Tapping a drill loads it, sends both switches and plays — the tap is the gesture a browser wants before it will make any sound at all. Moving a switch re-sends it straight away, with nothing else touched.
